Research philosophy

The platform separates descriptive evidence from conclusions. A high backtest return is not treated as a forecast. ML probabilities are model outputs, not guarantees. Optimization is presented as a mathematical baseline, not an allocation recommendation.

The goal is to make your research process more reproducible, inspectable and difficult to fool.

Quant Academy

Look-ahead bias

A historical decision must use only information that would have been available at that time. StockPulse shifts held positions before applying daily returns to avoid one common leakage path.

Overfitting

Searching more parameters increases the chance of fitting noise. That is why development data, unseen data, walk-forward testing and parameter stability matter.

Sharpe vs Sortino

Sharpe penalizes total volatility; Sortino focuses on downside volatility. Neither proves a strategy will work in the future.

Monte Carlo

StockPulse resamples the strategy’s realized daily return stream, including its trading logic and modeled friction. It explores path variability; it is not a forecast engine.

Regime analysis

A strategy can behave differently in trending, declining, sideways and high-volatility environments. Regime segmentation helps reveal dependency.

Research health

Train/test degradation, parameter instability, low trade counts and cost sensitivity are diagnostic signals to investigate—not automatic pass/fail labels.
